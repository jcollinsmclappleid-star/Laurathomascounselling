# Two Worlds. One Laura Thomas.
## Strategic research & website architecture
Therapist Website Studio | 3 November 2026 | Concept-stage strategy

### Recommendation
Keep both domains. Rebuild Laura Thomas Counselling as the professional home for counselling, Laura's background and publications. Introduce Parentology through an original, substantive gateway, then send visitors to its established website for specialist programmes, detailed services and resources. The strategic benefit is clearer audience journeys and a more coherent professional identity. Two domains alone do not create extra authority: each needs a distinct job, useful content and ongoing care.

Recommend Concept B, the editorial introduction, with the photographic pathway selection from Concept A developed into the signature transition. Introduce Laura before asking people to choose. Make both pathways and direct service navigation available immediately. Visitors arriving from Google on a service page should reach that service directly, without passing through a theatrical entrance.

A full current-site mobile-device audit, private analytics and Search Console access are outside this concept study. Proposed mobile layouts are supplied and tested separately.

### Laura's authority: evidence and boundaries

(Hons) Psychology; a humanistic/integrative counselling diploma; couples and young-person training; and MNCPS (Accred.). Her directory profile corroborates the broad service mix. These are source-verified public claims, not inspection of original certificates. Confirm the live professional register and permitted badge use before launch. Her qualifications page now describes an EdD in progress at Oxford Brookes, with developing interests in emotionally based school avoidance, parents' experiences and parent-school relationships. Her 2017 M.Ed. work concerned parents' experiences of multi-professional meetings in the EHCP process. Present her as undertaking doctoral study, never as holding a completed doctorate. Link research titles to the actual work; do not imply peer-reviewed publication without evidence. [R02, R14, R21]

The Parentology media page and Bedford Today carry international-publication information. The Bedford Today piece is a contributor article credited to Laura, dated 24 July 2026 in the retrieved article; Parentology's media listing gives a different date. It reports Mandarin publication of both books. Request Chinese-edition metadata and covers before displaying an edition badge. Do not amplify uncorroborated sales, print-run or bestseller claims. ### Two distinct audiences, with a helpful overlap
| Visitor need | First destination | Intended next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An adult wants space for anxiety, low mood or relationship difficulties | Counselling hub or individual service page | Read approach, practical information and fees; enquire |
| A couple wants therapeutic help | Existing couples page | Understand the process; make a couples enquiry |
| A family wants sessions involving family members | Existing family page | Understand suitability, participants and format; enquire |
| A teenager or parent is exploring the young person's therapy | Existing young-person page | Age-appropriate explanation of voice, consent, privacy and the assessment conversation |
| A parent wants specialist guidance between difficult moments | Parentology gateway | Understand the model; continue to the current Parentology service |
| A reader or journalist searches Laura or a book title | Author/publication pages | Verify authorship and details; explore related expertise or purchase |

Overlap is real, particularly around teens and family relationships. Add a short comparison block: young-person counselling focuses on support for the young person; parent support helps the parent think through their responses; family counselling concerns work with family members together. Confirm exact clinical boundaries, minimum age and referral arrangements with Laura. Let people switch pathways easily.

Create one consistent representation of Laura with links to her counselling practice, Parentology founder role, research and books. Each business should have its own organisation/service identity. Laura's Person identifier can be anchored to her counselling biography and reused as an absolute identifier by Parentology, once both sites can be edited. Publish helpful answers as visible server-rendered text, supported by relevant original pages and evidence links. Add explicit author/reviewer details, topic-specific headings, useful summaries and meaningful internal links. These improve understanding for people and search systems. Google's guidance confirms that established SEO foundations apply to its AI features; there is no required special AI schema or machine-readable AI file. [R28]

### Measurement: small enough to maintain
Record a pre-launch baseline from both verified Search Console properties: branded/non-branded query clusters, top pages, device split, index coverage, backlinks and existing book/service visibility. Preserve the export and exact dates.

Track counselling enquiry submissions, email/telephone clicks separately, calendar outbound clicks, Parentology gateway entries and cross-domain referral clicks. A click is not a confirmed appointment. Only count completed booking/enquiry events where the destination provides reliable confirmation. Use privacy-appropriate analytics and consent controls; do not send free-text enquiries, diagnoses or other sensitive details into analytics.

Review after launch weekly for technical/redirect issues, then monthly for useful landing-page traffic and qualified enquiries. Agree a baseline before setting targets. AI observations should record query, tool, date, geography and cited pages; repeat a small consistent query set. Treat examples as observations, not stable rankings. Google AI-feature activity is included in Search Console's Web reporting, rather than a clean standalone attribution report. [R28]

### Additional implementation decisions to settle
The individual counselling page currently lists £125 per session, while the fees page gives a starting point of £95. Treat these as different public statements requiring service-level confirmation, not evidence for a universal new fee. The concept avoids publishing a complete new fee schedule. The existing FAQ also contains inconsistent cancellation wording; Laura should approve one policy for all current booking pathways.

The public sitemap inventories and post metadata are included as CSV/JSON evidence in the handover. Sitemaps describe discoverability, not indexing or access entitlement. Some Parentology article URLs returned errors through the search retrieval service but were subsequently fetched successfully through a normal TLS-verified request. The /articles/schoolrefusal slug currently serves a research-led piece about parents being heard; its content should determine any future title or URL decision.
