## Evidence labels
“Verified” means a fact was checked against the named source; original certificates, full books, analytics and contracts were not inspected. Website claims, third-party directory corroboration, retailer edition data and contributor media reports are identified separately. Design recommendations and proposed topics are our judgement.

The 16-post/16-page WordPress inventory matches the two public content sitemaps. It does not enumerate private drafts, custom content types or every media object. The Parentology XML yielded 113 page URLs: 20 articles, 39 tag pages and 54 other entries. Their presence in XML is not proof of indexing, current offer status or entitlement to reproduce.

Desktop presentation on both live sites was visually inspected. Responsive markup/source was reviewed; the existing sites were not fully audited on physical mobile devices. The proposed mobile layouts were rendered and checked at 320px/390px. No ranking, search-volume, enquiry-rate or AI recommendation baseline was fabricated.

## Client approvals and supplied materials needed for production
- Current professional register entry, credential/badge wording and legal business/controller identity.
- Practice location and whether Parentology's Milton Keynes wording remains current. Do not invent a second counselling office.
- Service boundaries, ages, formats, fees, cancellations, availability, safeguarding/confidentiality wording and booking routes. Individual page lists £125; FAQ lists support from £95.
- Original portrait/practice photography, existing logos and cover artwork with reuse rights. Two supporting images are generated concepts; the interior is not her actual room and the family is fictional.
- English edition details and current companion-resource links. Full chapter contents were not available. Request the tables of contents/source excerpts before commissioning chapter-specific work.
- Chinese publisher/edition information and permitted covers before displaying international edition badges. The contributor media piece reports publication, but its date differs from the media page listing.
- Book/theme-led original articles, real Laura insight and any quotations before assigning authorship. The supplied expert article is an illustrative draft awaiting review.
- Blog export/media backup, credited guest-writer permissions, redirect decisions and preferred CMS workflow.
- Search Console/analytics access only during the agreed implementation engagement.

## Generated concept photographs
Built-in image generation produced the two supporting photographs. No existing Laura portrait or cover was AI-altered. The original photographs and the exact prompts used are included in the asset pack. Standard image format conversion was used for browser delivery.

Interior prompt: premium editorial architectural photograph of a modest, quiet English reading corner; olive linen chair, pale limewashed walls, oak, sheer curtain, ceramic cup, natural garden light; no people, text or brand; explicitly not Laura's real practice.

Family prompt: fictional British parent and approximately sixteen-year-old daughter in a warm lived-in kitchen; quiet natural conversation, terracotta/blue clothing, afternoon light and foreground foliage; no distress, text, logos or clinical scene; explicitly not real clients.
